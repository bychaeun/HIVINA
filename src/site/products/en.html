Design

Low Pressure Melamine

LPM · Melamine Paper

LPM is an eco-friendly surface material created by impregnating decorative paper with melamine and urea resins. Using only heat and pressure, it thermally fuses directly onto substrates like MDF, HDF, and PB, eliminating the need for extra adhesives.

Key strengths of the product

Various design expressions

Available in glossy, matte, semi-gloss, embossed, logo patterns, etc., enabling realistic and three-dimensional surface textures.

Excellent durability

Scratch, abrasion, heat, acid and water resistance: strong surface performance against external environments.

Eco-friendly material

Blocks harmful formaldehyde emissions from the board, safe for both people and the environment.

Cost competitiveness

Maintains functionality and quality while being more economical than alternative materials.

LPM Special

T-White LPM

Excellent opacity (hiding power). By adding special additives to standard white paper, T-White delivers excellent opacity in 100 gsm and 150 gsm grades. High gloss and high opacity for premium markets.

Coating LPM

Functional LPM that allows direct painting. Surface developed for poly coating: high gloss achievable with a single coating, minimal odor. Compatible with additional processes (high gloss, UV, etc.), reducing processing steps and costs.

Hi-Flex Low-Banding LPM

High elasticity with minimal warping. Reduces bending when pressed on one side; compatible with various backing materials (PET, UV, etc.).

Flame-Retardant LPM

Meets fire safety standards. Flame-retardant solution added during production; suitable for public and commercial spaces.

Inert Coating LPM

Additional eco-friendly coating applied on the standard LPM surface. Anti-fingerprint, high-gloss finish with self-healing scratch recovery.

Finishing Foil

Finishing Foil

Finishing foil is an eco-friendly, high-performance surface material using precision printing and water-based lacquer coating technology. It provides high-resolution design and excellent durability for furniture and interior substrates. Applicable to doors, bodies, moldings, as well as walls, ceilings, and decorative surfaces.

Pre Foil

Apply: furniture body and furniture back panel

Water-based lacquer coating on printed pre-impregnated paper. Base paper impregnated with a mixture of pulp and resin is water-based printed and then coated with a water-soluble lacquer. This surface coating method is applied by IKEA, a global furniture brand.

Post Foil

Apply: furniture door, tambour board, louver, etc.

General paper (60–100 gsm, approximately 3,000 designs from imported paper and Korean manufacturers) is impregnated with aqueous acrylic resin and then coated with water-based lacquer on the surface. Super Foil uses a special water-soluble resin during impregnation to improve adhesion, tensile strength, and surface properties.

UV Foil

Apply: door, louver, etc.

General paper is impregnated with a special water-soluble resin and then finished with UV coating. Types: matte / embossed.

Premium PET Foil

Apply: door, louver, etc.

General paper is impregnated with a special water-soluble resin and then finished with PET lamination. Types: PET UV Foil matte.
