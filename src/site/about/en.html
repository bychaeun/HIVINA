About us

30 years of Korean expertise, a global manufacturing hub in Vietnam

Backed by 30 years of technical expertise from Hyosan Group, Hi VINA JSC is a key global manufacturing hub in Vietnam established as a joint venture between Hyosan LPL and ICC.

Powered by in-house R&D and specialized design capabilities, we produce high-performance surface materials, including LPM and finishing foil. By combining strong cost efficiency, stable capacity, and strict quality control, Hi VINA JSC delivers trusted, sustainable solution to the global market.
