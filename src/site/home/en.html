About us

30 years of Korean expertise, a global manufacturing hub in Vietnam

Backed by 30 years of technical expertise from Hyosan Group, Hi VINA JSC is a key global manufacturing hub in Vietnam established as a joint venture between Hyosan LPL and ICC.

Powered by in-house R&D and specialized design capabilities, we produce high-performance surface materials, including LPM and finishing foil. By combining strong cost efficiency, stable capacity, and strict quality control, Hi VINA JSC delivers trusted, sustainable solution to the global market.

Our expertise

Eco-friendly interior materials

From melamine paper to finishing foil, laminate and tambour board.

01

LPM · Melamine Paper

LPM (Low Pressure Melamine) is an eco-friendly surface material created by impregnating decorative paper with melamine and urea resins. Using only heat and pressure, it thermally fuses directly onto substrates like MDF, HDF, and PB, eliminating the need for extra adhesives.

02

Finishing Foil

Finishing foil is an eco-friendly, high-performance surface material using precision printing and water-based lacquer coating technology. It provides high-resolution design and excellent durability for furniture and interior substrates. Applicable to doors, bodies, moldings, as well as walls, ceilings, and decorative surfaces.
